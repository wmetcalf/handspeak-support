HandSpeak is a free iPad app that learns one person's handwriting and speaks it aloud. It's made for people who can't speak but can write, including writers whose letters are clear to them but hard for standard handwriting recognition to read.

Getting started

  1. Speak tab: write a word or phrase with Apple Pencil or a finger. After a pause, HandSpeak reads it and says it out loud. Untrained words can work from the start using Apple's on-device handwriting reader.
  2. Train tab: add letters, common phrases or your own words with the + button, then write each one several times and tap Save. Five to ten samples each is a good start. Include messy versions, too.
  3. Corrections: if HandSpeak guesses wrong, tap the right choice (or Other… to type it). It learns from that drawing.
  4. Library: tap any item to see its samples, and delete any that are wrong.
  5. Tune: set the pause before reading, the voice and speaking rate, profiles for different writers, and backups.

Tips

  • Writing slowly or with a helper? Make the pause longer in Tune, or tap Pause timer on the Speak tab and Resume when finished.
  • A better voice: on the iPad, go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, download an Enhanced or Premium voice, then pick it in Tune. Personal Voice is supported too.
  • More than one writer: give each person their own profile in Tune › Profiles.
  • Moving to a new iPad: Tune › Backup › Back up, then save the file to Files or AirDrop it. On the new iPad, use Restore from a backup.
